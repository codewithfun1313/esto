Our Story

Fifteen years, six districts, one standard

EstateVista Properties opened in 2011 above a bakery on Meridian Street with two agents and eleven listings. The founding idea was unfashionable at the time: fewer listings, inspected properly, represented by someone who could answer any question about them without checking.

That standard has not changed as the firm has grown. We now handle more than 300 transactions each year across Downtown, Riverside, Green Valley, Palm Heights, City Center and Lakeside — still with a named agent responsible for every file from first call to handover of keys.

Around 62% of our business comes from repeat clients and their referrals, which is the number we watch most closely.
